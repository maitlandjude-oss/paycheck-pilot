How it decides

Overdue bills come first. Bills due before your next expected income are prioritized, with essential bills ahead of debt minimums and other bills when due dates are close. Bills due after your next income can be held. If available money cannot cover everything needing attention, Paycheck Pilot shows the shortfall.

Important: This is an organizational tool, not financial advice. It does not know your contracts, grace periods, late fees, credit consequences, shutoff rules, or personal circumstances. Confirm consequences with each biller before delaying a payment.
