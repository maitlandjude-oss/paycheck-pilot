## Prepared directory listing

Name: AI-Payer — Free Paycheck Bill Planner

Website: https://ai-payer.online/

Category: Finance

Short description: Got paid, but bills and everyday needs exceed your money? Protect groceries, gas and necessities, then organize required payments before your next payday.

Description: AI-Payer is a free browser-based tool built around “I got paid. What should I pay first?” Enter available money, your next income date, bills, minimum debt payments and everyday necessities. It sets aside the living amount you specify and organizes obligations into pay-now, hold-for-later and shortfall/review groups. Fictional example: $2,000 available, $500 for living needs, and $1,800 in bills due before payday leaves $1,500 for bills and a $300 gap to review. No bank connection or account is required to use the planner. Saved bill data stays in the browser. AI-Payer does not make payments, guarantee outcomes or replace individualized financial advice. Feedback and PayPal support are optional.
