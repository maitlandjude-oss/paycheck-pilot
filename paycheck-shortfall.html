You have $2,000. Needs and bills total $2,300. What would you pay first?

Payday arrived, but the money still does not cover everything before your next income. Groceries and gas are part of the decision, too.

The gap is $300. Paying every bill in full would leave less than the $500 this fictional household needs for daily living.

What information would change your answer?

Due dates alone do not tell the whole story. Which payment is overdue? Is there a shutoff or cancellation notice? What transport is needed for work? Is the next income reliable? Are payment arrangements available? These details can change the decision, and a planner cannot infer them.
