Know what you would get

How does it differ from the free planner?

The free planner organizes required payments around one next-income date. The kit adds a manual two-payday workspace, payment checklists, and downloadable plan backups. The free planner remains free.

Does it save automatically?

No. Your work stays in the open tab until you download a backup. Reopen that backup to resume. Backups are unencrypted files; keep them private and avoid account numbers or other sensitive identifiers.

Does it work on my phone?

A current desktop browser is recommended. Mobile opening, downloads, and printing vary by device. There is no installation or bank login.

Does it move money or give financial advice?

No. It is an educational organizer for your entered numbers. Check actual due dates, consequences, and payment arrangements directly with billers.
